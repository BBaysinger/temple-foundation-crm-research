# Common CRM Capabilities

This is a nonprofit-focused list of CRM capabilities to consider for current and future evaluation needs (including some features that may not be immediately required)

## Core Contact & Database Features

1. Contact records - ✅ Required
   - Individual profiles with core identity and engagement details.
2. Organization/company records - ⚪ Not Required
   - Profiles for businesses, partners, and institutional donors.
3. Household records - ⚪ Not Required
   - Group related contacts under shared household relationships.
4. Custom fields - ✅ Required
   - Add nonprofit-specific data fields beyond default CRM fields.
5. Tagging - ✅ Required
   - Apply flexible labels for quick categorization and filtering.
6. Segmentation - ✅ Required
   - Build targeted lists based on attributes, behavior, or history.
7. Lead status tracking - ⚪ Not Required
   - Track inquiry-to-supporter progression through defined stages.
8. Relationship mapping - ⚪ Not Required
   - Record relationships between people, households, and organizations.
9. Notes on records - ✅ Required
   - Store contextual notes from calls, meetings, and interactions.
10. File attachments - ✅ Required
    - Attach documents such as forms, letters, and agreements.
11. Contact timelines - ✅ Required
    - View chronological history of all activity for a record.
12. Interaction history - ❓ Don't Know
    - Log emails, calls, meetings, and other touchpoints.
13. Activity tracking - ❓ Don't Know
    - Track tasks, reminders, and completed follow-up actions.
14. Call logging - ❓ Don't Know
    - Record call outcomes, summaries, and next steps.
15. SMS logging - ⚪ Not Required
    - Track text-message outreach and responses per contact.
16. Email logging - ⚪ Not Required
    - Capture outbound/inbound email activity linked to records.
17. Social media profile linking - ⚪ Not Required
    - Associate public social profiles to contact records.
18. Contact merging - ⚪ Not Required
    - Combine duplicate records while preserving key data.
19. Duplicate detection - ⚪ Not Required
    - Automatically flag potential duplicate records for review.
20. Data import/export (CSV) - ✅ Required
    - Move data in/out using common CSV format.
21. Constituent self-service portal (profile + preferences) - ✅ Required
    - Let constituents update their own details and communication preferences.
    - (Website-dependent feature: generally delivered through website/portal integration.)
22. Householding automation rules - ⚪ Not Required
    - Automatically assign and maintain household groupings.
23. Communication consent history tracking - ⚪ Not Required
    - Keep auditable opt-in/opt-out consent records.
24. Field-level permissions - ⚪ Not Required
    - Restrict sensitive fields by user role.
25. Audit logs for record changes - ⚪ Not Required
    - Track who changed what and when.
26. Data retention and archival controls - ⚪ Not Required
    - Apply retention rules and archive inactive data.
27. Real-time duplicate prevention on form entry/import - ⚪ Not Required
    - Warn users of likely duplicates before save/import.
28. Role-based access control (admin vs staff) - ✅ Required
    - Enforce permission differences between administrators and staff users.
29. Record model flexibility for nonprofit edge cases - ⚪ Not Required
    - Support nonprofit-specific structures and edge-case workflows beyond standard donor/contact models.

## Donations & Fundraising

1. One-time donations - ✅ Required
   - Accept and record single gifts from supporters.
2. Recurring donations - ✅ Required
   - Set up and manage scheduled ongoing contributions.
3. Donation forms (embeddable) - ✅ Required
   - Embed branded giving forms on website pages.
   - (Website-dependent feature: requires website embed or CMS integration.)
4. Donation page builder - ⚪ Not Required
   - Create standalone donation pages without custom coding.
5. Peer-to-peer fundraising - ⚪ Not Required
   - Enable supporters to fundraise on behalf of the organization.
6. Campaign tracking - ✅ Required
   - Monitor gifts and performance by fundraising campaign.
7. Pledge tracking - ✅ Required
   - Record pledged amounts and track payment schedules.
8. Tribute donations - ⚪ Not Required
   - Capture gifts made in honor or memory of someone.
9. Employer gift matching tracking - ⚪ Not Required
   - Track matching-gift eligibility and submission progress.
10. Donation receipts (auto-generated) - ❓ Don't Know
    - Automatically issue receipts after completed gifts.
11. Tax receipt generation - ❓ Don't Know
    - Generate compliant year-end or per-gift tax statements.
    - Clarification needed: determine whether automated donation receipts alone are sufficient, or if formal tax-receipt generation is also required.
12. Offline donation entry - ✅ Required
    - Enter cash, check, and other non-digital gifts.
13. Fund allocation tracking (annual, monthly, endowment, critical solicitations) - ✅ Required
    - Assign incoming revenue to predefined categories for reporting and stewardship.
14. Grant tracking - 🟡 Possibly Required
    - Track grant opportunities, statuses, and awarded amounts.
15. Major donor tracking - ⚪ Not Required
    - Manage high-capacity donor relationships and activities.
16. Donor tiers - ❓ Don't Know
    - Classify supporters by giving level thresholds.
17. Giving history timeline - ⚪ Not Required
    - View chronological donation history per constituent.
18. Donor lifetime value calculation - ✅ Required
    - Estimate total long-term value of each donor.
19. Wealth screening integrations - ⚪ Not Required
    - Connect to tools that identify donor capacity indicators.
20. Donor segmentation - ✅ Required
    - Group donors for targeted stewardship and appeals.
21. Soft credits and gift attribution tracking - ⚪ Not Required
    - Attribute credit to influencers, households, or related contacts.
22. Matching gift workflow automation - 🟡 Possibly Required
    - Automate reminders and follow-up for match submissions.
23. Recurring gift failed-payment recovery workflows - ⚪ Not Required
    - Trigger retries, alerts, and outreach after payment failures.
24. Major gift moves management pipeline - ⚪ Not Required
    - Track cultivation stages, asks, and next actions.
25. Planned giving / legacy gift tracking - ✅ Required
    - Record and steward bequests and planned gift intentions.
26. Grant deadline and reporting workflow reminders - 🟡 Possibly Required
    - Set reminders for grant submissions and required reports.
27. Donation payout reconciliation - ⚪ Not Required
    - Reconcile processor payouts with recorded gift transactions.
28. Donation acknowledgement automation (beyond receipts) - ✅ Required
    - Send configurable thank-you and stewardship acknowledgements after gifts.
29. Revenue categories (by program/fund/campaign) - ✅ Required
    - Categorize revenue by program, fund, and campaign for stewardship and finance reporting.

   - Set up event records with dates, locations, and event details.

   - Publish branded pages with event information and registration links.
3. Event ticketing - ❓ Don't Know

5. Early bird pricing - ⚪ Not Required
   - Apply discounted pricing for early registrations.
6. RSVP tracking - ✅ Required
   - Record attendee responses and registration status.
7. Guest list management - 🟡 Possibly Required
   - Manage attendee rosters, statuses, and check-in readiness.
8. Check-in tools - ⚪ Not Required
   - Verify attendance on-site during event entry.
9. QR code ticketing - ⚪ Not Required
   - Generate and scan QR-based tickets for faster entry.
10. Waitlist management - 🟡 Possibly Required
    - Maintain waitlists and promote attendees as spots open.
11. Table assignment - ⚪ Not Required
    - Assign attendees to tables or seating groups.
12. Sponsorship tracking - ⚪ Not Required
    - Track sponsor commitments, benefits, and fulfillment status.
13. Event capacity limits - ✅ Required
    - Enforce registration caps to prevent overbooking.
14. Event reminder automation - ✅ Required
    - Send timed reminders before events automatically.
15. Event feedback surveys - ✅ Required
    - Collect post-event feedback from attendees.
16. Attendance tracking on contact record - ⚪ Not Required
    - Store each contact’s attendance history for future segmentation.
17. Event revenue tracking - ✅ Required
    - Report ticket, donation, and sponsorship revenue by event.
18. Virtual event integrations - ⚪ Not Required
    - Connect events with webinar or livestream platforms.
19. Multi-session event support - ⚪ Not Required
    - Manage events with multiple sessions or breakout tracks.
20. Event analytics reporting - ⚪ Not Required
    - Analyze registration, attendance, and conversion metrics.
21. Mobile event check-in (phone/tablet) - ⚪ Not Required
    - Check in attendees using mobile devices on-site.
22. Offline check-in with sync - ⚪ Not Required
    - Continue check-in without internet and sync later.
23. Automated post-event follow-up workflows - 🟡 Possibly Required
    - Trigger thank-yous, surveys, and next-step outreach after events.
24. Event registration forms/workflows - ✅ Required
    - Publish registration flows and capture attendee details for events.
25. Automated event confirmations - ✅ Required
    - Send registration confirmations and key event details automatically.

   - Connect CRM actions to external tools using no-code automations.
5. API access - ⚪ Not Required
   - Programmatically read/write data for custom integrations.
6. WordPress/CMS integration - ⚪ Not Required
   - Embed forms and connect website content workflows.
7. Webhook support - ⚪ Not Required
   - Send real-time event notifications to external systems.
8. Scheduled accounting sync and export mappings - ⚪ Not Required
   - Automate periodic exports and map fields for finance systems.

## Operational & Budget Fit

1. Monthly platform cost fits target budget - ✅ Required
   - Verify base plan plus required modules aligns with monthly budget constraints.
2. Low ongoing admin overhead - ✅ Required
   - Confirm a small team can operate core workflows without heavy CRM administration.
3. New-user onboarding usability - ✅ Required
   - Validate that staff can run core tasks after reasonable onboarding.
4. Initial setup/config/deployment simplicity - ✅ Required
   - Evaluate initial setup effort, required configuration depth, and deployment complexity.
5. Scalability switch risk - ✅ Required
   - Estimate the likelihood of needing to switch platforms as organizational complexity grows.
6. Data migration complexity - ⚪ Not Required
   - Assess effort and risk of migrating historical data into or out of the platform.
7. Workflow automation depth (multi-step, branching) - 🟡 Possibly Required
   - Support multi-step, conditional, and branching automation beyond basic triggers.
8. Sandbox/testing environment availability - ⚪ Not Required
   - Provide safe environments for testing workflows, integrations, and configuration changes.
9. Backup/export resilience (full-fidelity exit path) - ⚪ Not Required
   - Ensure complete, usable data export paths for backup and vendor-exit scenarios.
10. Support quality + implementation partner ecosystem - ⚪ Not Required
    - Consider vendor support quality and availability of experienced implementation partners.
